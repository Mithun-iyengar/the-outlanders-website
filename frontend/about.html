OUR STORY & JOURNEY

We craft safe, memorable, and environmentally responsible experiences that connect adventure enthusiasts with the wild beauty of nature.

As a registered adventure and travel company, The Outlanders has been creating experiences across diverse destinations, from Western Ghats treks and weekend getaways to customized trips and outdoor adventures.

We also conduct specially planned trips and adventure programs for schools, colleges, and corporate groups, combining exploration, teamwork, learning, and unforgettable memories.

With institutions such as BRV and PES University, Bengaluru among our recurring clients, we continue to build lasting relationships through well-organized and engaging experiences.

With 8,000+ happy customers and counting, our journey is driven by one simple belief — every adventure should be safe, meaningful, and worth remembering.
